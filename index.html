About Railwise

This is a demo. Tickets made here are for practice only and cannot be used to travel. To book a real ticket, use IRCTC.

Timetables marked Verified timetable (12951 and 12952) list every halt with arrival and departure times and distance, taken from published schedules. Partly verified means only some stops are confirmed. Times unverified trains come from my own knowledge and may be wrong. Fares are rough, unverified amounts for an adult, and sources disagree on them, so check IRCTC before booking. Timetables change, so confirm on NTES or IRCTC before you travel.

Railwise lists 12 well-known services across 7 routes. Seat availability is not shown because it needs a live railway feed.

Railwise is a demo project and is not affiliated with Indian Railways or IRCTC.
